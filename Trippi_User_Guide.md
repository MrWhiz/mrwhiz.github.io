# Trippi - Your Ultimate Group Trip Companion 🚗💨

Welcome to **Trippi**! Whether you're planning a weekend getaway with friends or a cross-country road trip, Trippi keeps everyone in sync with real-time location tracking, smart expense splitting, and seamless trip coordination.

This guide provides a comprehensive overview of Trippi's features and step-by-step instructions on how to use them.

---

## 🏗️ Getting Started

### Creating a Trip
1. Open Trippi and tap the **"Create Trip"** button.
2. Enter your **Destination** and select a trip type if applicable.
3. Once created, you'll receive a unique **Trip Code** (e.g., `TRIP-A1B2C3`).
4. You are automatically the **Owner** of this trip.

### Joining a Trip
1. If a friend has already created a trip, tap **"Join Trip"**.
2. Enter the **Trip Code** shared with you.
3. You'll instantly be added to the trip and can see all updates.

---

## 👥 Member Management

### Roles
- **Owner**: The creator of the trip. Has full control, including deleting the trip and managing all members.
- **Organizer**: Promoted by the Owner. Can manage guest members, vehicles, and itinerary.
- **Participant**: Can add expenses, update their location, and view trip details.

### Guest Members (No-App Support) 👤
Trippi allows you to include friends who aren't using the app yet:
1. **Add Guest**: Organizers can tap "Add Guest" in the **Members Tab**.
2. **Splitting**: Guests can be included in expense splits just like regular members.
3. **Claiming**: If a guest joins the app later, they can tap **"That's me"** next to their guest name to merge their account and take over their recorded expenses.

---

## 💸 Expense Tracking & Splitting

Never argue about "who owes what" again.

### Adding an Expense
1. Go to the **Expenses Tab** and tap **"Add Expense"**.
2. Enter the **Amount**, **Category** (Food, Fuel, Stay, etc.), and a brief **Description**.
3. The person logging the expense is the default **Payer**.

### Smart Splitting
- **Equal Split**: By default, expenses are split equally among all trip members.
- **Custom Splits**: You can choose specific members to include or exclude from a transaction.
- **Settlement Summary**: View the "Balances" section in the Members Tab to see who is "in the green" and who needs to pay back.

## 🚘 Vehicles & Occupancy

Coordinate who is driving and who is riding.

### Managing Vehicles
1. In the **Vehicles Tab**, Organizers can add cars/bikes participating in the trip.
2. Assign a **Lead Driver** for each vehicle.

### Occupancy
- Assign members to specific vehicles so everyone knows which car they are in.
- Useful for tracking fuel expenses per vehicle and ensuring nobody is left behind!

---

## 🗓️ Itinerary & Pit Stops

Plan your journey markers.

### Adding Stops
1. Use the **Itinerary Tab** to plan your route.
2. Add **Pit Stops** for fuel, food, or sightseeing.
3. Mark stops as **Completed** as you progress through your journey.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips for Users
- **Share the Code**: Send your Trip Code via WhatsApp or SMS to get everyone onboarded quickly.
- **Stay Active**: Keep the app in the background during the drive to ensure your location remains updated for your friends.
- **Clear Balances**: Use the "Settle Up" feature (if available) at the end of the trip to archive transactions.
